About Paddle Shore

Pickleball in Semmancheri.

Paddle Shore is a pickleball venue at 2/118, MGR Street, Nehru Nagar, Semmancheri, Chennai — 3 courts, booked online through TurfTown.

The Venue

Made for playing.

Paddle Shore has 3 pickleball courts with synthetic flooring in Semmancheri, Chennai. The courts are non-AC, and non-marking shoes are a must.

Players rate it 5.0 out of 5 on TurfTown, across 6 ratings. Ratings come only from people who have played at the venue.
